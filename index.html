Welcome to the website of Carnegie Mellon University’s All University Orchestra. The AUO is a student music organization committed to providing CMU students, faculty, and alumni an outlet for an orchestral music experience. For more information about our organization, please contact AUO Music Director, Dr. Jeffrey Klefstad at cmuauo@gmail.com.
